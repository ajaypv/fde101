**Abstention** is the system choosing not to provide a substantive answer when it lacks sufficient evidence, permission, or confidence.

## Tiny example

If retrieval finds no policy for a requested country, a useful response is “I cannot verify that from the available policy documents” plus an escalation path—not an invented universal policy.

## Why it matters

A system that answers every question can look helpful while failing dangerously on unanswerable cases.

## FDE note

Include answerable and unanswerable cases in evaluation. Track both false answers and unnecessary refusals, then set the decision threshold according to the cost of each failure.
